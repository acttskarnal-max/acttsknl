FAQ

Questions students ask.

What are the fees?

There are no course fees. Hostel and food are free for eligible ACTTS students. Call us to check eligibility.

Is hostel and food really free?

Yes, under Kaushal Aashiyana, for eligible ACTTS students. Contact the centre to confirm eligibility and available seats.

How long are the courses?

The Robotics course is 4 months and the Advanced IT course is 6 months.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, you receive an NSDC certificate after completing the course.

Is placement support available?

Yes, the centre provides placement support after the course. Call us for current details.

What are the centre timings?

Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Closed on Sunday.

Where is the centre?

Govt. ITI Campus, Kunjpura Road, Karnal, Haryana 132001.
